Tournament Overview

Tournament Format: Pool Play Round Robin Style, with top teams advancing to the elimination bracket.

Match Rules: Games to 11, Win by 2. In tournament play, teams switch sides of the court after a team reaches 6 points to keep it fair due to elements like wind and sun.

Options & Guidelines for 50-Team (100-Player) Tournaments

For a 50-team (100-player) pickleball tournament, the best approach is to divide the field into 8 to 10 pools of 5-6 teams each, with 2-3 top teams from each pool advancing to a single-elimination bracket. This structure optimizes court usage and guarantees at least 4-5 matches, providing a high-quality experience for participants.

Recommended Structure (50 Teams)
  • Pool Setup: 10 pools of 5 teams (allows for efficient 4-5 matches per team) or 8 pools of 6–7 teams.
  • Advancement: Take the top 2-3 teams from each pool to form a 16 or 24-team single-elimination bracket (depending on court availability and time).
  • Game Format: Single games to 15 (win by 1 or 2) are ideal for large round-robins.
Key Considerations
  • Time Management: Pool play with 50 teams takes time. Ensure you have enough courts—aim for at least 12-16 courts to avoid long waits.
  • Tie-Breakers: With many pools, use point differential or head-to-head results to determine tie-breakers for bracket seeding.
  • "Best of" Scenario: To maximize "bang for your buck," allow 4 teams to advance from each pool to a larger bracket if you have time, or a shorter, higher-stakes bracket if time is tight.
